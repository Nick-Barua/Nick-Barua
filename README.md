## 🔬 Research Focus

> **33.0% fatality rate.** Pedestrians lying on the road die at more than double the rate of upright pedestrian collisions — yet no current ADAS standard is designed to detect them.

My research addresses this critical and largely unresolved gap in road traffic safety: the detection and injury-risk modelling of pedestrians in non-upright postures — individuals already lying on the road surface due to medical emergency, intoxication, or displacement following a prior collision. Standard ADAS systems achieve only a **21.4% True Positive Rate** for detecting these individuals under night conditions — a 73.3 percentage-point classification gap.

My programme bridges **Forensic Biomechanics**, **Multi-Modal Sensor Fusion**, and **Functional Safety (ISO 26262)** to move from post-mortem analysis of fatalities to proactive harm prevention in autonomous driving systems.
